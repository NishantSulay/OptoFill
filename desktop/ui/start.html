Open your EMR to start

  1. Type your EMR’s web address in the bar above and sign in as usual. OptoFill opens it here next time.
  2. Set it up once from the panel on the right: click each box and say what it is.
  3. Dictate, check the values, fill. Ctrl+Alt+D (⌘⌥D on a Mac) starts and stops dictation from anywhere.
