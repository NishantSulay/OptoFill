How It Works

From dictation to a filled exam form

Works in your browser on web-based EMRs, with no IT integration. You set each EMR up once.

🔌
1

Install & Add Your Key

Add OptoFill to Chrome and paste your Anthropic API key in Settings. The key is stored only on your computer.

🧩
2

Set Up Your EMR Once

Open your exam screen and start setup. Auto-map suggests what each box is from its row and column labels; click any box to correct it.

🎙
3

Dictate

Click the OptoFill icon to open its side panel, click the mic and speak your findings the way you normally would. Leave out patient names and other identifiers.

🧠
4

Claude Reads the Dictation

Claude turns your words into standard exam values — VA, refraction, IOP and more. It only sees your dictation, never your EMR page.

✅
5

Fill, Check & Undo

OptoFill puts each value in the box you set up, reads every box back to confirm it took, and lists anything that didn't. One click undoes the fill.

Features

Built for the way optometrists dictate

Less typing, with you in control of every value that goes into the record.

🎙

Natural Dictation

Speak in plain clinical language. Mid-sentence corrections ("eighteen — sorry, nineteen") and shorthand like "left eye same as right" are understood.

🧠

Optometry-Aware Extraction

Claude is instructed in optometry notation — sphere, cylinder, axis, add, VA, IOP, cover test — and records only what you said. It never fills in normals you didn't dictate.

🌐

Works With Web-Based EMRs

Set up any browser-based EMR in minutes, including exam forms split across tabs or embedded in a frame. No integration from your EMR vendor needed.

📋

Checked Fills, One-Click Undo

Values only go into boxes you've set up. Each box is read back after filling, anything that didn't take is flagged, and Undo restores the form exactly.

🔒

Clear About Your Data

No OptoFill servers and no analytics. Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to Google to transcribe it, and the text goes to Anthropic. Read exactly what goes where.

⚡

Quick Fill Before Setup

On an EMR you haven't set up yet, Quick fill lets Claude match values to boxes from the page's labels — then set the EMR up for dependable fills.
