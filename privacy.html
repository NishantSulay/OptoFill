Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: October 14, 2026

Short version: OptoFill has no servers and does not collect, store, or sell any data. Your API key stays on your device. When you dictate, Chrome's speech recognition sends your audio to Google to turn it into text, and when you fill, that text is sent to Anthropic (Claude). Do not dictate patient names or other identifiers.

1. Who We Are

OptoFill ("we", "our", "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps optometrists and eye care professionals document patient exams faster using voice dictation and AI. OptoFill is developed and maintained by OptoFill.

Contact: privacy@optofill.com

2. What Data OptoFill Handles

a) Anthropic API Key

  • You provide your own Anthropic API key in the extension's Settings panel.
  • This key is stored locally on your device only using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API.
  • It is never transmitted to OptoFill servers. It is sent only to api.anthropic.com as part of the AI request you initiate.

b) Voice Dictations / Transcripts

  • Speech is transcribed by Chrome's built-in speech recognition (the Web Speech API). Chrome sends your audio to Google's speech service to do this; it is not transcribed on your device. OptoFill itself never receives, stores, or sends audio.
  • The resulting text transcript is sent to api.anthropic.com when you click "Fill Fields."
  • The current transcript is kept in Chrome's session storage so it survives closing the side panel, and is cleared when you clear it or close Chrome. OptoFill has no servers and keeps no copy.

c) Form Field Metadata

  • Once you have set up your EMR in OptoFill (see below), only your dictation is sent to Anthropic. Claude turns it into standard exam values, and OptoFill places them into the boxes on your own computer.
  • If you use "Fill Fields" on an EMR you have not set up yet ("quick fill"), OptoFill also sends a description of the visible form fields — their labels, field names, input types, and nearby text such as row and column headings — so Claude can guess which box to fill. Nearby text can include whatever is printed on screen near a box, which may include the patient's name or other details shown on the page. Setting up your EMR avoids this: after setup, only your dictation is sent.
  • This data is not stored by OptoFill and is not transmitted to any service other than Anthropic. OptoFill never sends screenshots of your screen.

d) EMR Setup

  • When you set up an EMR, OptoFill saves which box holds which exam value (for example, "this box is Amplitude OD") along with the box's on-page label and position text. This is stored only on your device via chrome.storage.local, separately for each website.
  • The setup contains no patient data and is never transmitted anywhere.

e) Custom Instructions

  • Any custom instructions you enter in Settings are stored locally via chrome.storage.local and sent to Anthropic's API as part of the system prompt when you process a transcript.

3. What OptoFill Does NOT Do

  • Does not operate any servers or databases that store your data.
  • Does not collect analytics, usage statistics, or telemetry.
  • Does not track which websites you visit.
  • Does not share any information with third parties other than Anthropic (for AI processing when you click "Fill Fields"). Your audio reaches Google through Chrome's own speech recognition, described below.
  • Does not sell, rent, or monetize user data in any form.
  • Does not record or store audio.

4. Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude AI)
When you click "Fill Fields," a request is sent to api.anthropic.com containing your transcript and form field metadata. This request is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy and API terms, including how long Anthropic keeps API data. If Claude declines a quick-fill request, Anthropic may retry it on another Claude model; the data still goes only to Anthropic.

Google Chrome Web Speech API
Voice transcription is performed by Chrome's built-in speech recognition, which sends your audio to Google's servers to be transcribed. OptoFill does not control how Google handles that audio. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Patient Privacy Laws (HIPAA, PHIPA and Others)

OptoFill is a software tool. It is not a covered entity, business associate, or health information custodian. If you use OptoFill with patient information, you are responsible for making sure your use complies with the laws that apply to you — for example HIPAA in the United States, or PHIPA and other provincial health privacy laws in Canada. This page is not legal advice.

  • Do not dictate patient identifiers such as names, dates of birth, health card or insurance numbers, addresses, or phone numbers. Clinical findings (VA, refraction, IOP, and so on) are all OptoFill needs.
  • Your audio is sent to Google by Chrome's speech recognition, and your dictation text is sent to Anthropic. Check whether your obligations allow this, and whether you need an agreement with either company, before using OptoFill with real patients.
  • Anthropic offers agreements for handling health information to some API customers. Contact Anthropic to find out whether one is available for your account.
  • Set up your EMR rather than relying on quick fill, so that text shown on the page is not sent.

6. Data Retention

OptoFill retains no data on its own. Data stored locally (API key, custom instructions, session transcript, EMR setup) persists only in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time by:

  • Clearing your Chrome extension storage (Settings → clear API key)
  • Removing the OptoFill extension from Chrome
  • Clearing Chrome's site data for the extension

7. Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Allows OptoFill to interact with the tab you are currently viewing when you click the extension icon. Required to read and fill form fields on your EHR page.
  • storage — Allows local storage of your API key and settings on your device only.
  • scripting — Allows OptoFill to load its page script into your EHR tab if it isn't there yet, and to find which part of the page (including embedded frames) holds the exam form.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — OptoFill must work on any web-based EHR system, and practices use different platforms at different URLs, so the extension cannot list domains in advance. OptoFill's page script loads on every page you visit (including embedded frames) so it is ready when you need it, but it does not read, fill, or send anything from a page until you use OptoFill on that page — set it up, dictate, or click "Fill Fields."

8. Children's Privacy

OptoFill is intended for licensed eye care professionals. It is not directed at or designed for use by individuals under the age of 18.
