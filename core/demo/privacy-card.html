Clear about where your data goes

No OptoFill servers. Here is everything that leaves your computer, and where it goes.

Stays on your computer

🔑 Key, settings & setup

Your Anthropic API key, custom instructions and EMR setup are stored only in Chrome on your computer.

Sent to Google

🎙 Your voice

Chrome's built-in speech recognition sends your audio to Google to turn it into text.

Sent to Anthropic

🧠 Your dictation text

When you click Fill, the text goes to Anthropic's Claude to read out the exam values. It never sees your EMR page once it's set up.

Before sending

🕶 Identifiers removed

Names after cues like "Mr." or "patient", dates, ID and phone numbers, emails and addresses are stripped from the text. Still, don't dictate names.

Never

🚫 No tracking

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. OptoFill stores nothing about you or your patients.

Your call

⚖️ Your obligations

You're responsible for meeting the health privacy laws that apply to you, such as HIPAA or PHIPA. Use test patients until you've checked.
